Principles

How we build

The rules every atlapps app follows.

Runs on Atlassian

Built on Forge with no outside servers. Your data stays in Atlassian's cloud, in your site's region.

Read-only first

Apps ask only for the permissions they need. Ledgerstone never changes anything in Jira.

Fair, simple pricing

Free for small teams, then a low per-user price through the Marketplace. No sales calls.
