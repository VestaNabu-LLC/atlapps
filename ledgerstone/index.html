Getting started

From install to your first audit in about five minutes. You need to be a Jira administrator.

  1. InstallInstall Ledgerstone on your Jira Cloud site from its Atlassian Marketplace listing. It asks only for read-only Jira permissions. Sites with up to 10 users are free; larger sites start with a 30-day free trial.
  2. Open itIn Jira, go to Settings (the gear icon) → Apps and choose Ledgerstone in the sidebar. The first time, Jira asks you to let Ledgerstone read Jira as you; accept to continue.
  3. First auditWith no audit on record, Ledgerstone takes the first snapshot as soon as the page opens ("Running your first audit"). It takes a few seconds. After that the audit runs on its own once a week.
  4. Fix and re-checkStart with High findings. After a fix, Refresh re-reads everything from Jira, and Run snapshot now runs the weekly audit immediately and adds the result to the trend.

Documentation

What each part shows

OverviewA verdict line; counts of High findings, Medium findings, items that could not be verified, and objects scanned; findings by area, each with an Open link to its tab. Below it, the weekly trend: the last 12 runs and what changed since the previous one.
Permission schemesEvery permission scheme and the projects that use it, then each flagged grant: the permission and who it is granted to.
Filters & dashboardsSaved filters and dashboards shared publicly or with every logged-in user, with their owner and share settings.
Roles, groups & usersProject role assignments, administrative and default-access groups with their members and inactive accounts, and account counts by type.
Access checkThe same minimal reads made as the app (the weekly job) and as you (this page), so you can see what Ledgerstone can read and spot any gap in coverage.
RatingsHigh: public, admin-level for everyone, or an inactive admin. Medium: every logged-in or licensed user. Could not verify: an area that was not fully read, for example after a time limit.
LicenceWithout an active licence or trial, Ledgerstone shows "Ledgerstone is not licensed on this site" with a link to Manage apps, and reads nothing from Jira.
Data and uninstallLedgerstone stores counts and configuration names (schemes, filters, dashboards, groups, roles) for its last 12 runs, in Atlassian's cloud with your site's data residency. People's names are shown live and never stored. Uninstalling deletes everything it stored.
