Ledgerstone: Permission Audit for Jira

See who can see and change what in Jira. Read-only. Runs on Atlassian.

Ledgerstone gives Jira administrators a one-page answer to "is our Jira more open than we think?". It reads permission schemes, shared filters and dashboards, project roles, groups and accounts, flags anything reachable by anonymous visitors or by every logged-in user, and tracks the trend week over week.

Why admins use it

  • Findings, not data dumps. One verdict line, with High and Medium findings you can act on.
  • Weekly trend. An unattended weekly run keeps the last twelve results and shows what changed.
  • Runs on Atlassian. Read-only scopes, no data leaves Atlassian, no third parties.
